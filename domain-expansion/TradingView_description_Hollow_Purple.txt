[b]█ HOLLOW PURPLE · NY PM[/b]

The New York afternoon, built from ICT's Price Action Chronicles on the market-on-close (MOC) macro and tested on [b]6,333 sessions[/b] of NQ, ES and RTY (2018–26). Use a chart of 5 minutes or less for the final hour.

[b]What it draws[/b]
• [b]The PM range[/b] (13:30–13:59), shaded, with its high, low and CE. The NY PM session opens with the 13:30 pre-market, and its first 30 minutes play the part 09:30–10:00 plays in the morning.
• [b]The read at 14:00:[/b] where the 13:59 close sits in the range, with the odds of which side breaks first.
• [b]The −0.5 ARP[/b] (algorithmic range projection: half the range beyond the high or low) and [b]1, 1.5 and 2 SD[/b], both sides, 14:00 to 16:14.
• [b]The first break[/b] after 14:00, labelled at its candle.
• [b]The final-hour range[/b] (15:00–15:49), drawn as it forms and set at 15:50, then [b]its −0.5 ARP[/b] above and below.
• [b]The MOC macro[/b] (15:50–16:00), shaded.

[b]Card[/b] (13:30 → 17:00): the top row, [b]PLAN[/b], says what to watch now: before the break, the side the 14:00 read favours and its edge; after it, the next level on that side not yet reached, its distance and its odds by 16:14; from 15:50, the final-hour side the MOC is likely to take, then its ARP; after 16:14, the day's summary. Below it: the PM range against ADR20, the 14:00 read, the break and whether it held, the ARP and SDs on the break side with ✓ or the live odds by 16:14, then the final-hour range with the odds of which side the MOC takes first, what the MOC took, and the final-hour ARPs.

[b]Layout[/b]: Desktop shows every row. Mobile (View settings) keeps PLAN, READ, BREAK and MOC on the Hollow Purple card and the key rows on the London card, and hides the recent-sessions table. Default places: London card top right, Hollow Purple bottom right, recent sessions bottom left.

[b]What the tests showed[/b] (each held in 2018–24 and 2025–26 and on each symbol)
• [b]The 13:59 close sets which side breaks first.[/b] % up first by octant, bottom to top: 12 · 19 · 29 · 44 · 55 · 67 · 79 · 89. Broken by 16:14 on 99.5% of sessions; the first break held on 52%.
• [b]On the first-break side by 16:14:[/b] −0.5 ARP 74%, 1 SD 51%, 1.5 SD 35%, 2 SD 23% (2025–26: 69 · 45 · 30 · 18).
• [b]The MOC macro[/b] (15:50–15:59) took one side of the final-hour range on 72% of sessions (to 16:14: 80%), and the side nearer price first. It reached the final-hour −0.5 ARP on 16% (to 16:14: 27%).
• [b]Live odds:[/b] from a level's distance in ATR15 (the 15-minute ATR at 13:30) and the time left. Fit on 2018–24, scored on 2025–26: Brier 0.116 → 0.092. In 2025–26 levels traded up to 8 points less often than predicted.

[b]Tested and not found[/b]
• The −0.5 ARP is not a terminus: MOC runs stopped there no more often than at −0.375 or −0.625.
• MOC lows and highs did not land on the day's octant, 16th or quadrant more than on a shifted grid (they were reached 61 / 49 / 40% of the time when price sat inside them at 15:50).
• Long in the tooth: MOC direction was 50 / 50 against the day's trend.
• Smooth equal highs or lows were taken less often than the jagged side.
[i]A map of the afternoon with honest odds, not a list of turning points.[/i]
